# Edith-style content cuts

Use this when Carlos asks for cuts like the Edith content.

Rules:
- Do not add title cards, hook titles, or title frames.
- Do not add Gumercindo robot badge or decorative moldura unless Carlos explicitly asks.
- Burn subtitles into the video.
- Select complete viral/strong speech segments; do not cut a thought in the middle.
- Clips may exceed 60 seconds and can be up to 1m30s when Carlos allows it.
- Prioritize practical demonstrations of Gumercindo/IA, sales automation, agent execution, and “super funcionário” framing.
- Verify rendered clips visually before delivery: subtitles readable and not covering the speaker’s face.
- Deliver the whole lot, not a sample: one clip per `MEDIA:` line (or the manifest listing every id and path)
  plus a ZIP and a contact sheet. Handing over only a few previews makes him ask where the rest are.
- Caption that goes with each post: the theme/hook line plus one short CTA to the link in bio — **no hashtags**
  unless he asks for them.
- These lots go out through a per-platform queue (Instagram + YouTube, one post every ~30 minutes) with the
  pause/resume-at-a-requested-time mechanics described in `instagram-api-publishing-operations`.
- Title/length are per-request, not a fixed house style: this class means **no title and no title frame by
  default**, but confirm per lot — his other cut batches carry a hook title for the first seconds.
